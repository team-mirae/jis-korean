JIS Korean on a MacBook

Mode A, JIS Korean's original layout, on a MacBook's built-in US keyboard seen as a JIS keyboard through Karabiner-Elements: カタカナ, 英数, かな and ろ on a tap of the modifiers, Caps Lock as Control, and a Fn layer on 🌐 and right ⌥ with ¥ (README.md). The keyboard itself stays US ANSI, so it types as printed whenever Karabiner isn't running.

カタカナ · 英数 · かな
Tap left Control for Korean, left ⌘ for English, right ⌘ for Japanese. Held, they're Control and ⌘ as usual.
ろ on right Shift
A tap types ろ (\ and _ in Mode A), where JIS has it: right of /. Held, it's Shift.
Caps Lock is Control
Left Control is busy with カタカナ. Caps Lock itself is fn+Tab.
The caps move by one
JIS has @ [ ] where ANSI has [ ] \, so the MacBook's [ ] \ type Mode A's ' " · [ 「 · ] 」 (む in Kana), and ' types JIS's : key. ` is left alone.
F6–F10
한글, 한자, 두벌식, 전각 and 반각 on fn + the top row, or fn + 6 to 0. Shift+Z is F10 too.
Without Karabiner
The keyboard is still US ANSI to macOS, so a password typed before Karabiner starts comes out as printed.
Fn

Fn layer

Hold 🌐, or right ⌥
Function keys Navigation, keypad, media and ¥ Nothing
The HHKB US Fn layer
The same layer as on the HHKB US setup, with ⌘⇧4 on I and Play/Pause on P, plus what fn does on any Mac: forward Delete, and Home, End and the Page keys on the arrows.
¥ on -
In place of F11: ¥ and | in JIS English, ー in Kana, — and ^ in Mode A. = has nothing: F11 and F12 are on the top row.
Two Fn keys
🌐 tapped alone still does what System Settings sets for it. Right ⌥ is a second Fn, where JIS keyboards have one.
