JIS Korean on an HHKB US

Mode A, JIS Korean's original layout, on an HHKB Professional BT (US) seen as a JIS keyboard through Karabiner-Elements: カタカナ, 英数, かな and ろ on a tap of the modifiers, ¥ and JIS's ] on the keys the HHKB has to spare, and Backspace top right and a second Fn on right ◇, as on an HHKB JIS (README.md).

カタカナ · 英数 · かな
Tap left ◇ for Korean, left ◆ for English, right ◆ for Japanese. Held, they're ⌥ and ⌘ as usual.
ろ on right Shift
A tap types ろ (\ and _ in Mode A), where JIS has it: right of /. Held, it's Shift.
¥ and JIS's ]
The HHKB's \ is ¥ (— and ^), left of Backspace as on JIS. Delete is JIS's ] (] and 」, む in Kana), the key an HHKB US lacks.
Backspace top right
On `, as on an HHKB JIS. Fn+` is still forward Delete. An alternative rule keeps Delete as Backspace and puts ] on `.
The caps move by one
JIS has @ [ where ANSI has [ ], so the HHKB's [ ] type Mode A's ' " and [ 「, and ' types JIS's : key.
Right ◇ is Fn
Held, it's a second Fn key, where an HHKB JIS has one. F6–F10 (한글, 한자, 두벌식, 전각, 반각) are Fn+6 to Fn+0, and Shift+Z is F10 too.
Fn

Fn layer

Hold the HHKB's Fn, or right ◇
Function keys Navigation, keypad and media Nothing I and P: the Mac extras version (the standard one in small: Print Screen, Pause)
Two Fn keys
The HHKB's own Fn is in its firmware. Right ◇ is a second one, made by Karabiner, where an HHKB JIS has its Fn. It sends the same keys as the HHKB US Fn layer, so the layer doesn't depend on the firmware.
Mac extras, or standard
One version of the rule puts ⌘⇧4 on I and Play/Pause on P; the other keeps Print Screen and Pause, which macOS mostly ignores.
F6–F10
한글, 한자, 두벌식, 전각 and 반각 on Fn+6 to Fn+0, from either Fn.
